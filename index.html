The Brew & Bean Journey

Founded in a quiet brick alley by two lifelong coffee explorers, Brew & Bean began with an uncompromising mission: to treat coffee as an art form rather than a fast commodity.

Every single bean is responsibly sourced directly from shade-grown estates in the Western Ghats. We small-batch roast every Wednesday and Saturday in our vintage cast-iron roaster to lock in notes of dark cocoa, dried stone fruit, and delicate wildflower honey.
